Photomosh is one of the coolest online image editing things I've ever seen.

With as little as four clicks, you can turn a boring image into a GIF with vintage TV effects. It gives images this reclaimed, thrift-store feel. I used it to make little GIF avatars for my [Medium account](https://medium.com/@jeffreyhumble):

![photomosh glitched image subtle cinemagraph](/images/0b8ba70f-85d8-4f8c-ac51-7e25db83beb2/MOSHED-2018-9-4-14-41-58.gif)

![photomosh glitchy static avatar image](/images/1eecf934-624c-436b-882d-4f32e3a1b890/MOSHED-2018-9-3-12-54-22.gif)

![photomosh vintage image camera effect](/images/1ae4fb4e-b5c2-41b3-8942-38770a38252e/MOSHED-2018-9-3-12-52-40.gif)

Simply drop an image in there and click "Mosh" and you're done. You can save the live animation as a static JPG or as a GIF. You can even use your webcam to record with a glitch effect right from your browser!

[![photomosh image glitcher and GIF creator](/images/663a7509-8f7d-42d8-851e-3bff7627c8a8/Screen-Shot-2018-09-05-at-17-51-44.png)](https://photomosh.com)

Try it out: <https://photomosh.com/>
