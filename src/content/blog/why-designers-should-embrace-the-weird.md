It feels like digital design all looks the same.

For years

- The Challenge, everything looks the same

  - Example
  - The strategic side
  - ~~The Death of the CDO, flattened orgs - being ashamed, cosplaying business people~~
  - Rise of AI, tactical fading, strategic rising
  - Designers know what it feels like to be the weird ones
- The Strategy - when everybody zigs, you zag

  - Example
  - It doesn’t have to make sense.

    - How it makes sense. Lateral Thinking
    - Example of designer’s way of diverging, Double Diamond
- The Examples

  - Find the extreme users.
  - Switch up the process.

    - design it to be as bad as possible
    - start at the end,
  - Challenge conventions

    - listen for assumptions
    - do things differently from the competition
  - A little chaos never hurt anyone.

    - divergent process examples (spiral, constraints)
    - randomness and evolution
    - make it ugly on purpose to break the flow
  - Find your users’ subculture. - Discord
  - Encourage lateral thinking. - recharging the team’s creative muscles, helping users to explore, helping them to love the process
- Examples of what it’s not

  - Kid stuff
  - Cosplaying the artist
  - Perfectionism
- Objection: Won’t this slow things down?

  - Know when to get weird - wicked problem, innovation, hacking, down time
  - Know when to hammer it out - move fast, predictable projects
- Objection: Won’t this make me less business-like?

  - Know when to blend in - during budget cuts, when you want to switch out of design, when you sense annoyance, calling it something else
  - Know when to stand out - diminishing returns, nothing else is working, you think you can measure the effect, you’re in a safe space
- AI for the boring, humans for the spicy bit
