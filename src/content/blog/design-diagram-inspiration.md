For the last 15 years, I’ve been collecting diagrams that I found useful for my job, and I’m sharing all of them with you.

As a designer and strategist, distilling complex ideas into a diagram is like the hokey pokey…that’s what it’s all about. So, what is a diagram?

<p class="text-large">A diagram is a simplified drawing showing the appearance, structure, or workings of something; a schematic representation.</p>

I use diagrams as inspiration when I’m writing an article, making a presentation, or designing workshops. Diagrams are great jumping-off points for any type of creative work!

## Where these diagrams come from

I started collecting these images from presentations, PDFs, and websites back in 2011. I never planned to share them, so I stored them in a private Dropbox folder for years. Many are screenshots, but I tried to name the file with the author’s name to keep track of attribution.

In 2023, I discovered are.na, which automatically tracks the source of images. This tool gave me new inspiration for archiving these useful diagrams, and I still keep up with this collection today.

Follow the [are.na board](https://www.are.na/jeff-humble/diagrams-about-design-creativity-and-life) behind this collection to stay updated when I add diagrams. You can also access the sources of these diagrams for attribution purposes, but a Google reverse-image lookup is probably quicker.

## How to remix diagrams ethically

Next time you need a new diagram, use this eclectic mix of diagrams, models, memes, and frameworks to communicate your own complex ideas.

Here’s how I re-purpose diagrams:

1. Drop a diagram you like into a design tool like Figma
2. Cover all of the original text with boxes.
3. Redo the diagram’s non-verbal imagery with your own drawing and imagery
4. Remix the imagery until you have evolved beyond the original diagram
5. Do a reverse-image search of the original diagram on Google
6. Add the title and author of the image source to your diagram with something like, “Adapted from _____________”

You might also upload these to an AI tool and have it remix the diagram to meet your needs. But don’t forget to credit the original author as inspiration!
