This year, I went a little crazy with the Spirit of Halloween meme format.

I could only find three online, and that just wouldn't do. So, I made nine of them 🫢

Now, you have no excuse for showing up to work without a costume.

## Spirit of Halloween Costume Memes

### 1. UX Designer Costume Meme

![halloween costume meme ux designer](/images/0c596067-5963-4f09-bab7-f3e9202be836/Halloween_Costume-UX_Designer-Jeff_Humble.jpg)

Making this one was too easy. How are there so many clichés in the UX world?

### 2. Service Designer Costume Meme

[![halloween costume meme service designer](/images/700bbe0c-73eb-44a2-a3a9-1b8fff6ebcd2/Halloween_Costume-Service_Designer-Jeff_Humble.jpg)](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jeff-humble_halloweencostume-designmeme-servicedesign-activity-7255549138128302080-p15J?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop)

I made this meme for the most underappreciated role in design...the service designer. If you see someone wearing this costume, hug them immediately.

### 3. Strategic Designer Costume Meme

[![halloween costume meme strategic designer](/images/e40ef9ae-866b-442f-9886-9381e0c819be/Halloween_Costume-Strategic_Designer-Jeff_Humble.jpg)](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jeff-humble_halloweencostume-designmeme-strategicdesign-activity-7256681472508469248-M7nb?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop)

I made this meme, but it really made itself because Strategic Designers have so many clichés. If you're curious about the role, [​learn more about strategic design here​](https://www.thefountaininstitute.com/blog/what-is-strategic-design).

### 4. Growth Designer Costume Meme

[![halloween costume meme growth designer](/images/387a9d06-98b8-44c5-b9cc-9be38e8e724a/Halloween_Costume-Growth_Designer-Jeff_Humble.jpg)](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jeff-humble_halloweencostume-designmeme-growthdesign-activity-7257044236317958145-Eyp6?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop)

Growth Designers get a lot of hate because they tend to be so marketing-driven, but we need designers who aren't afraid of numbers. If you're interested in growth design, check out [​my guide to testing and experiments​](https://www.thefountaininstitute.com/blog/getting-started-in-testing-and-experimentation-a-designers-guide) for designers.

### 5. Staff Designer Costume Meme

[![halloween costume meme staff designer](/images/a8646aba-2577-43db-8cc1-a5654605766f/Halloween_Costume-Staff_Designer-Jeff_Humble.jpg)](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jeff-humble_halloweencostume-designmeme-staffdesigner-activity-7255217064322248704-Aqty?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop)

Just say "no" to managing kids! Staff Designer positions are rising after the lockdown, so with this costume, you, too, can experience the joy and pain of being the team's "mature" designer.

### 6. UX Architect Costume Meme

[![halloween costume meme ux architect](/images/a7a620ec-55f6-4530-9ce1-55d31738da35/Halloween_Costume-UX_Architect-Jeff_Humble.jpg)](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jeff-humble_halloweencostume-designmeme-uxarchitect-activity-7257406373305454592-3pqa?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop)

I've always wondered how you get these magical roles like UX architect, but now you know it's the label maker. Re-live the good 'ole UX days when people let you organize the product according to the users' needs rather than the feature factory Frankensteins we have today.

### 7. Design Manager Costume Meme

![halloween costume meme design manager](/images/335fb751-aad8-4225-ac3e-d6638d017290/Halloween_Costume-Design_Manager-Jeff_Humble.jpg)

This one leaves a trail of greasy fingerprints wherever they go. You've never worked for an agency if this image doesn't trigger you.

### 8. UX Strategist Costume Meme

![halloween costume meme ux strategist](/images/10d96f3c-7e94-4f2d-92a3-2442cf3570ed/Halloween_Costume-UX_Strategist-Jeff_Humble.jpg)

I designed this costume around the mystical notebook that (supposedly) guides the UX team, but nobody can understand it. This is probably the easiest costume, but the job is perhaps the hardest. If you want to pierce the veil of mystery, check out [my course to become a UX Strategist.](https://www.thefountaininstitute.com/defining-ux-strategy)

### 9. Entry-Level Design Job Costume Meme
